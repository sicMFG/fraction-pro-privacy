Fraction Pro Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Fraction Pro is a construction measurement calculator designed to help users perform fractional calculations, convert measurements, record job notes, and manage calculation history.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Fraction Pro Android app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Fraction Pro provides calculator, conversion, job notes, and calculation history features.

Information entered into these features is processed and stored locally on your device, where applicable.

Fraction Pro does not require you to create an account or provide your name or email address to use the calculator.

The developer does not operate a server that collects or stores your job notes or calculation history.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Fraction Pro integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to support advertising.

Google's advertising technology automatically collects and shares certain information, which may include:

  • IP address, which may indicate approximate location
  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention, subject to its applicable policies and user settings.

During development and internal testing, Fraction Pro may display test advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK remains integrated into the app.

For more information, review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Policies

3. How Information Is Used

Locally entered measurement and note information is used to provide the app's requested functionality.

Information processed through Google's advertising services may be used to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, maintain service security, and prevent fraud.

4. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell your job notes, measurements, or calculation history.

Advertising-related information may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described in Section 2.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored job notes and other saved app information remain on your device until deleted through available app controls, cleared through Android's app storage settings, or removed by uninstalling the app, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Information collected through Google services is subject to Google's applicable data retention and deletion policies.

You may contact the developer about privacy-related questions or requests using the email address below.

6. Data Security

Fraction Pro uses local device storage for its calculator-related saved information.

Security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and its operating system.

Third-party advertising services apply their own security practices to the information they process.

7. Children's Privacy

Fraction Pro is intended as a general construction utility and is not specifically designed for children.

The app is not intended to knowingly solicit personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, advertising services, or data practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.
